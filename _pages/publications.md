Theses
======

- Bachelor's Thesis (in Chinese): [双星-极端质量比旋进的引力辐射](../files/Bachelor_Thesis_YuchengYin.pdf) (Gravitational radiation of b-EMRI systems)<br>
  Chapter 2 and 3 of this thesis are the **First** detialed reviews of black hole perturbation theory and EMRI waveform modeling written **in Chinese**. I hope it can provide a tutorial for beginners. Chapter 4 and 5 are about our work on b-EMRI waveform [*arXiv:2410.08673*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2410.09796). The first section of chapter 6 about inhomogeneous Sasaki-Nakamura formalism is now avaliable, see [arXiv:2511.08673](https://arxiv.org/abs/2511.08673). We also extended the calculation to generalized Sasaki-Nakamura formalism, see [arXiv:2512.07937](https://arxiv.org/abs/2512.07937).
